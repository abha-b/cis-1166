Help with studying and access

Help with files, videos, or accessibility
  • Links open in the same tab. Use your browser’s Back button to return to the course page.
  • If Google Drive asks you to sign in, use your Temple account. If access is still denied, send the instructor the topic name and the link.
  • To study offline, use Google Drive’s Download option if the file owner permits it. Do not repost or share restricted course files.
  • Use the video player’s captions and playback speed controls when available. Captions or transcripts have not been verified for every video.
  • Some slide PDFs may not work well with screen readers. If you need captions, a transcript, accessible notes, or help reading a diagram, contact the instructor with the section and page or timestamp.
  • You can zoom the page using your browser. No account is needed to read this website.

TUJ Accessibility Services can help arrange academic accommodations.

When asking a question, include the section number, slide page or video timestamp, and what you have tried. You do not need to send grades or personal medical details to report a broken link.
